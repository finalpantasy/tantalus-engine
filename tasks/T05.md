## Remaining work

Compare the real field with an original reference, including depth, alpha/transparency, animation state, projection, and camera behavior. Resolve the separate blank synthetic-3D path. Real gameplay, model presentation, and skinned animation remain pending.

The capture warmup counts received GPU frames rather than main-thread updates. Before that correction, identical commands could produce either content or only the clear color; earlier transform-only blank conclusions were not reliable evidence. Final source-centered, sequential-atlas, selected-camera captures are repeatable, but this is not a parity or broad hardware compatibility claim.

## Evidence history

- `FieldMapper` maps real BGS/BGI data into renderer-independent camera/overlay/sprite/navigation contracts. Real mapping is recorded in `audit/field-mapping.json`.
- `ClientSceneAdapter::apply_field` validates atlas handles and rectangles, filters inactive sprites, spawns stable depth-ordered textured ECS sprites, and preserves native sprite dimensions separately from atlas resolution.
- AssetBundle metadata resolves the selected field's actual atlas path ID, and its decoded RGBA matches UnityPy exactly. `BundledField` now supplies that same identity and image to inspection and capture; no placeholder atlas remains in the real-field path.
