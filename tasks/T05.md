## Remaining work

Correct real field origin/projection, camera/overlay filtering, alpha/transparency, and atlas mapping against a reference capture. Resolve the separate blank synthetic-3D path. Real gameplay, field/model comparison, and skinned animation remain pending.

The capture warmup now counts received GPU frames rather than main-thread updates. Before that correction, identical real-field commands could produce either valid content or only the clear color; earlier transform-only blank conclusions were therefore not reliable evidence. Two consecutive post-fix captures are stable, but this is not yet a broad hardware compatibility claim.
