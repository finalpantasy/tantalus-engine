## Outcome and scope

Implement classic, same-scene, and prepared-arena encounters through one coordinator with explicit readiness stages, resource leases, participant ownership, transition/commit/return lifecycle, cancellation, and stale-completion rejection. This is synthetic infrastructure; real camera and asset integration remain pending.
