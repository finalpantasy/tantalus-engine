# REVIEW 2026-11-16 — battle conformance milestone

## What exists now

The battle subsystem has a six-corpus conformance matrix, all committed
and gated:

| Corpus | Policy | Path | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| battle-run-all-patterns | armed level-20 party | report | 1002 V / 38 D / 0 E |
| battle-world-run-all-patterns | armed | world hand-off | identical twin |
| battle-run-all-patterns-weak | unarmed level-8 | report | 460 V / 544 D / 0 E |
| battle-world-run-all-patterns-weak | weak | world hand-off | identical twin |
| battle-run-all-patterns-flee-first | flee key, no attacks | report | 3 V / 387 D / 593 Esc |
| battle-world-run-all-patterns-flee-first | flee-first | world hand-off | identical twin |

Every corpus is 562 real scenes × all patterns (1054 battles) run against
the owned install's `p0data2.bin` / `p0data7.bin`. The three pairs are
bit-identical on the gated fields (outcome / gil / EXP / error); only
auxiliary fields (ticks, drop rolls) vary where they should.

## How to operate it

- Post-landing triage (one command):
  `tantalusctl assets inspect-bundle --path p0data2.bin
  --battle-run-everything <out dir> --scripts-bundle p0data7.bin`
  Regenerates all six corpora in one process and prints each against its
  committed baseline. A clean tree prints `identical=true` six times.
- Gate: `tantalusctl audit summary --deny-fail` — six corpus rows plus
  three cross-path identity rows (divergence = fail, unit-tested both
  directions in `audit_summary` lib tests).
- Single modes (`--battle-run-all`, `--battle-world-run-all`) remain for
  targeted work; knobs: `--all-patterns`, `--battle-seed` (default
  0x5EED; 0xBEEF cross-seed verified), `--battle-weak-party`,
  `--battle-flee-first`.

## What was proven

- Two independently wired engine paths (report runner vs world hand-off
  with decided-defeat gate, exactly-once reward commit, HP/MP carry-back)
  agree bit-for-bit across all outcome families: dominant victories,
  mass defeats (weak mode), escapes (flee mode), and the five fog
  battles that legitimately reach no outcome.
- The weak mode earned its keep: it exposed two real cross-path bugs
  (battle-script party-list mismatch `&[0]`/`&[]`, and demo units
  missing defensive stats the world path had) that armed mode could
  never surface — 31 deltas, fixed to 0.
- Reference semantics for battle-script party binding were pinned from
  the pinned Memoria source (ObjTable vanilla links, btl_init CharacterId
  scan, InitObject 251-254 via partyUID) and delivered citation-backed;
  Claude's partyUID implementation landed conformance-clean (only
  `ticks` moved on 96/1054 rows).
- Capacity: ~37 s cold / ~21 s warm for the full armed matrix — about
  20 ms per battle including parse (absolute numbers, no comparison
  claim).

## Known limits (carried)

- Defeat *presentation* timing is still the machine owner's follow-up;
  outcomes are proven, animation pacing is not.
- Item/card drops are independent draw streams per path — deliberately
  not cross-compared.
- Chain-engine (field-battle) corpus comparison deferred: its player
  construction is richer/reference-shaped by design, so three-way
  identity is characterization work for its owner.
- Audit debt: CLOSED. `audit summary --deny-fail` is green end-to-end;
  documented causes (zero-state dry-run stops with ledger causes, WATCH
  in unloaded world scripts, install-missing clips and silent movies)
  are derived from the reports and do not count as failures. Anything
  new and undocumented turns the gate red again.
